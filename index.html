Research programme

Questions I work on

Six connected themes, linked by a commitment to transparent, reproducible and policy-relevant methods.

  1. 01

    Road safety & driver behaviour

    Crash-severity modelling, risky driving and riding intention, fatigue and the working conditions of professional drivers.

    Crash data · JD-R · MRBQ
  2. 02

    Explainable AI for transport

    Interpretable machine learning that shows why a model predicts risk, and tests whether those explanations are stable.

    XGBoost · SHAP · LIME
  3. 03

    Large language models in transportation

    Mapping how LLMs are used across the field and building LLM-assisted tools for analysis, policy and decision support.

    LLMs · RAG · Bibliometrics
  4. 04

    Intelligent transportation systems

    Adaptive traffic-signal control, IoT, edge AI and microsimulation for safer and more efficient networks.

    SUMO · VISSIM · TinyML
  5. 05

    Travel behaviour & technology adoption

    Why people adopt Mobility-as-a-Service, e-mobility and new transport modes, especially in emerging economies.

    PLS-SEM · fsQCA · Mode choice
  6. 06

    Sustainable mobility

    EV charging equity, public transport and emissions policy, evaluated through transparent scenario analysis.

    EV equity · Transit · Policy

Explainable AI

Results in Engineering · Volume 32 · Article 112401

Explainable machine learning for binary casualty prediction: Evidence from New South Wales

Four tree-based ensembles model casualty outcomes across 92,189 NSW crashes. XGBoost achieved an AUC-ROC of 0.839, while SHAP analysis tested the stability of model explanations.

Mobility as a Service

Journal of Public Transportation · Volume 28 · Article 100176

Willingness to adopt mobility as a service in a pre-deployment context

Evidence from Bangladesh using a dual-method PLS-SEM and fsQCA framework. The model explains 70.8% of willingness and highlights performance expectancy, platform trust, risk, and privacy.

Rider behaviour

Transportation Research Part F · Volume 118 · Article 103552

Investigating risky riding behaviors among young motorcyclists in Bangladesh

A modified motorcycle rider behavior questionnaire reveals five behavioural factors. Traffic errors predicted near-crashes and crashes, while education, engine capacity, and exposure shaped risk.

MRBQMotorcycle safetyBangladesh

Driver fatigue

Transportation Research Part F · Volume 122 · Article 103789

The trapped driver phenomenon: risky driving intention among professional drivers in Bangladesh

An extended job demands–resources model shows that fatigue-driven exhaustion links adverse working conditions to risky driving intention, with economic vulnerability and scheduling pressure emerging as major demands.
