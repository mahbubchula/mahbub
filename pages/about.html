Biography

Mahbub Hassan (Graduate Student Member, IEEE) is a Transportation Engineering researcher pursuing his Master of Engineering in Civil Engineering at the Department of Civil Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, Chulalongkorn University, Thailand, under the supervision of Prof. Kasem Choocharukul, Ph.D., currently holding a First Class standing with a CGPA of 3.81/4.00. He holds a Bachelor of Civil Engineering with Honours from Universiti Malaysia Perlis (UniMAP), Malaysia, where he graduated with Dean's List honors in five semesters and received the Best International Student Award.

His research sits at the intersection of transportation engineering and artificial intelligence — spanning intelligent transportation systems, connected and autonomous vehicles, road safety and driver behavior, sustainable mobility, explainable machine learning, and large language models applied to real-world transportation problems.

He is the Founder and Research Team Leader of B'Deshi Research Lab, an international research group he built from the ground up that has produced 25+ peer-reviewed journal and conference publications in ITS, road safety, machine learning, and sustainable mobility. Through the lab, he has mentored 20+ undergraduate researchers to co-authored publications in venues including Nature Portfolio, Elsevier, Springer Nature, MDPI, Frontiers, and IEEE.

As an author and co-author of 20+ published journal articles and 16+ conference papers (with several more accepted or in press), he has presented his work at international conferences across Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, India, Indonesia, and Saudi Arabia. He also serves as an invited peer reviewer for 21 international journals, having reviewed 31+ manuscripts to date.

He is a Graduate Engineer registered with the Board of Engineers Malaysia (BEM), a Graduate Student Member of IEEE, and a Student Member of the American Society of Civil Engineers (ASCE). His work is supported by the Chulalongkorn University ASEAN and Non-ASEAN Countries full graduate scholarship, and has been recognized with a Best Paper Award at ICSM 2025 (SAE International Malaysia), among other honors.

Beyond publishing, he builds and open-sources AI-powered transportation tools — including clickSUMO, ParkSense-AI, UrbanFlow AI, TransitGenie, and EcoMoveAI — and produces educational content on research and engineering for his YouTube channel, reaching 1,000+ subscribers and 100,000+ views.
