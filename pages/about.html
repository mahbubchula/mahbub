Biography

I am a Transportation Engineering Researcher at Chulalongkorn University in Bangkok, Thailand, focusing on sustainable mobility and AI applications in transportation systems.

My research journey combines my passion for intelligent systems with urban mobility challenges. I have published Q1 journal papers, including being the first academic to analyze Malaysia's newly released GTFS datasets for transit network efficiency assessment in Johor Bahru and Penang.

Currently, I am developing a real-time digital twin platform for Malaysian transit systems, combining GTFS data with SUMO microsimulation for RapidKL bus networks. I am also building SUMO-Forge, a web-based platform to democratize traffic simulation by abstracting SUMO's technical complexity.

I believe in making research accessible through open-source tools. I have developed multiple AI-powered transportation analysis tools including EVChargeAdvisor-AI, ParkSense-AI, and PolicySim-AI.
