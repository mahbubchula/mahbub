B'Deshi Emerging Research Lab

Founded in September 2023, B'Deshi Emerging Research Lab is an international, mostly online research group focused on intelligent transportation systems, road safety, machine learning, and sustainable mobility. The lab mentors undergraduate researchers from Bangladesh and collaborates with institutions across Southeast Asia, South Asia, and the Middle East.
